Typering Rules

OFFICIAL RULES

Khafati Match

To start a Khafati match, the opponent must reply to 10 Namusi messages. Counting in the group is invalid. Counting must be done in private messages. You must be recording while counting. The opponent can start the count without sending insults first. Spam, auto-clear, self actions, timers, being inactive for more than 3 minutes, Alsamaq, editing or deleting text, and actions by admins in a group owned by one of the participants result in a loss. There is no limit to Balakha, but each Balakha requires 10 Namusi messages to be performed. Example: X challenges Y, and Y accepts. If the rules are followed, the match is carried out under the agreed conditions. Resending is allowed a maximum of 3 times, and proof must be provided. Recording is not required during a Khafati match, except while counting and when proof of a 3-minute absence is needed.

Starting a Count

To start a count, 4 Mahvi words must be specified. For example: Sex, Lash, Va, Khar. The count runs from 1 to 0. The opponent must reply to the number 0 and state one condition. Failure to follow that condition results in a loss. Counting in a group is invalid. A count performed without recording is invalid. Starting a type or requesting a match during a Khafati match is invalid.

Brief Explanation of a Fight Match

To start a Fight match, a request must be made to the opponent through a Checker. Both sides enter the tunnel and follow the rules. The profile must match the relevant side. A period of 10 to 20 minutes is allowed for setting up the profile. The Judge must be online. If one participant goes offline, the Judge must be informed with proof so the situation can be checked. If the Side Owner goes offline, the entire side loses. No loss is assigned to the whole side until the Owner goes offline.

General Typering Rules

There are several types of Typering matches: Rhythmic and Story-based. In Rhythmic Typering, Larz is allowed. Story-based Typering must be performed without Larz. The Checker either follows the time agreed upon by both sides or sets a time between 10 and 30 minutes. During this period, both participants type and must be recording. For example, if the duration is 20 minutes, both recordings must cover the full 20-minute period. Larz: Negative points. Emojis: Negative points. Spam: Negative points. Attacker: Direct loss. Alsamaq: Direct loss. Auto: Direct loss. Media and insults before a match or during ordinary conversation result in an immediate identity penalty (Sik). The Checker may forgive this up to 3 times.

Story-Based and Rhythmic Typering

In Story-based Typering, you must use a punchline and the text must have a story structure. It should not consist only of repeated phrases. Rhythmic Typering without Larz must follow a consistent rhythm. Example: Kas Mas Chas Khar L.Sh — Kas Mas Chas Khar L.Sh The rhythm must not be interrupted, and Larz must not be used. Full-Larz Rhythmic Match: Larz is allowed in this type. You must still follow the rhythm. Half-Larz Rhythmic Match: A small amount of Larz is allowed, but it must not become excessive. Do not use numbers, English letters, or various unnecessary symbols in Rhythmic Typering. Each negative point deducts 2 lines. At the end, the Judge reads the text and counts the mistakes. The participant with the lower score loses.

Larz Match

A Larz match is held by agreement and with a Judge present. The participant who issued the challenge must announce how many Mahvi points have been set as the losing limit. For example, if the limit is 4 Mahvi, receiving 4 Mahvi results in a loss. The number of lines must also be specified. For example, 2 lines below 2 text lines. Failure to follow this condition results in a warning, and 3 warnings result in a loss. In a Larz match, spacing does not cause a loss if it is not excessive. However, your typing must remain organized. Do not leave excessive gaps, or you may receive a warning. The words used in a Larz match are: ~{Sex Chas Kas Mast Khar Lash Va Sag Shal Va Val Lash}~ Only these words may be used. Do not change their spelling. Changing their written form counts as Larz. A match request can be made together with a Julan or a Type. Editing text or deleting a message results in an immediate identity penalty (Sik). You must be recording from beginning to end. Auto-clear and Alsamaq result in an immediate identity penalty (Sik). There is no absence countdown in a Larz match. You specify the Mahvi limit and the number of text messages yourself. Escaping from the match for any reason counts as a loss.

Touch Larz

According to the rules of the match above, you send the request to the opponent privately as a Julan or a Type. When both participants are ready, the Checker provides a text of approximately 5 to 10 lines. Both participants must already be recording. After both participants confirm that they are ready, the Checker sends the text. Both participants must type the text in the group without any Larz and send it. Important: Even one Larz makes the text invalid. The participant who sends it later loses. This match consists of 3 to 5 rounds. The process is repeated 3 or 5 times. Whoever sends the better text wins, and the loser's Sik goes to the winner. The match can be for the Sik of all identities, for a loss, or for the Sik of a locked identity. In this match, Alsamaq and Auto result in an immediate Sik. The text must match the Checker's text exactly. Any Larz or missing word results in a loss. The Checker must be valid and must be active in at least one court. The Checker's text must also be free of Larz; otherwise, the Checker makes the match invalid. Julan against Julan is immediately invalid. Type against Type is immediately invalid. Not having a locked identity makes the match invalid.

Fight Match Rules

The following actions result in an immediate loss during a Fight match: 4 instances of spam, 3 instances of emoji use, media, hanging code, escaping from the match, editing text, deleting text, and Alsamaq. The Judge must close the group and make only those with a set profile and the Side Owners admins — but without permissions. I repeat: without permissions. After that, the match starts at the agreed time. A Fight match can be for the Sik of an identity if both participants agree. Using a Type or Julan during a Fight match results in an immediate Sik. There is no such thing as a timer in a Fight match. Once the match starts, you can continue typing with a timer for as long as you want. However, if your account gets banned, you have only 20 minutes to return with a new account. You are not allowed to send media during a Fight match unless the Checker gives permission.

Julan Guidelines

Your Julan must have 2 types. Your Julan must include a time and a tunnel link. Your Julan must be announced through the King, and the King must be private. Before sending the Julan to the opponent, the Checker must pin it in the Julan group. Coordinate with the second participant when they arrive. If the second participant joins and the Julan and Checker are not present in the tunnel group, the tunnel and Julan are invalid. Insulting before the match results in an immediate Sik. Your Julan must follow the rules of at least one court. Your Julan must be judged by a valid Checker who is active in a court. Saying "my friend is a Checker" or "someone I know is a Checker" is not accepted. Most of the match rules must be included in the Julan. Otherwise, it is invalid. It must contain at least 10 points. If a Julan is declared invalid, you are not allowed to send another Julan for 24 hours. If you use a fake identity for a Julan and the Checker proves it, the Sik of all your identities goes to the opponent. For Participant 1, you must have the Creator, Owner, and Khalegh locks. A hidden seen status is not a loss. Seeing the message and not joining is a loss. A valid Checker must be present in the tunnel for the Julan. For example, it is not acceptable to send a Julan and have the Checker join only after the opponent arrives. The Checker and Julan must already be pinned in the tunnel. A tunnel with fewer than 100 members is invalid for all types of Typering, but it is acceptable for Khafati. If Participant 1 sends a Julan but does not have the identity lock written in Participant 1's details, they lose and the identity receives a Sik. If you do not know the opponent's identity, write: {Private message of the person who was forwarded}

How to Send a Typering Type

You go to the opponent's private messages and say: "I challenge you to a match with a 3-minute AFK rule, under the rules of [name of court]." The request can be sent using an identity or the private message of the person who was forwarded. If the opponent replies, they are required to participate. If they do not reply, it is up to them whether they accept or reject. They are only required to participate if they reply. If they do not reply, there is no obligation to play, and they can simply accept or reject the request. The Type must be valid. Do not invent invalid or made-up Types, such as the so-called new-generation matches.

Endurance Match

The following actions result in a loss during an Endurance match: Media, more than 3 emojis, a timer, auto-clear, spam, Alsamaq, being disconnected more than 3 times during the match, hanging code, and using a Type or Julan during the match. Being on record is mandatory, and active sessions must be limited to one session. In an Endurance match, both participants enter the tunnel and start their match under the supervision of the Checker. If they type manually and follow the rules, the match continues until one participant has been AFK for 3 minutes. The 3-minute AFK period is calculated as follows: For example, Participant X sends a text at 12:00. If they do not send another text by 12:04, they lose. The 3-minute period consists of 12:01, 12:02, and 12:03. The match must be performed manually. Editing text, deleting text, auto-clear, self actions, timers, and Alsamaq all result in an immediate loss. A Judge must be present for an Endurance match. In an Endurance match, the Sik goes to the loser. The Type or Julan must specify whether the Sik applies to all identities or to the identity used in the match. Running out of phone battery, running out of internet, going to school, going to the gym, or any other excuse for leaving the match is not accepted. The only exception is if your phone shuts down. In that case, you have 15 minutes to return and provide proof. The Endurance match follows the specified time. Whatever time has been agreed upon, you are required to participate. Otherwise, you lose. A Creator lock is mandatory. You must either have a recording or have it locked. There is no counting in an Endurance match, and Larz is allowed. There is no Typering. If you switch to text mode and become AFK, that is not our responsibility.
